INTERPRETATION

Serine 438 is a primary S1 pocket contact residue. The S438T substitution
introduces a gamma-methyl group (Thr vs Ser) into the S1 binding pocket,
which experimental data (Andersen et al., 2009) shows causes a ~320-fold
increase in Ki for (S)-citalopram (escitalopram).

The computed Delta G of -0.197 kcal/mol does NOT replicate this experimental
magnitude. This "missing clash" is attributed to:

  1. Rigid-receptor approximation: the ligand reorients to avoid the methyl
     group without triggering the desolvation and entropic penalties that
     manifest in the living transporter.
  2. Na1 coordination disruption: S438 coordinates a sodium ion in the S1
     site. Replacing it with Thr likely disrupts this water/ion network in a
     way Vina's scoring function does not capture.
  3. Scoring function noise: the 0.197 kcal/mol shift is below Vina's
     reported accuracy floor (~0.5 kcal/mol).

The experimental data in Andersen et al. (2009) remains the gold standard.
These results establish a rigid-docking baseline and confirm the correct
mutation site (438) is within the S1 pocket.
